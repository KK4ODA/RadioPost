Write every reply by the "How to write" section of this repository's CLAUDE.md. Where a
repository has none, use the same rules from ~/.claude/CLAUDE.md. For replies in particular:

- The first sentence answers the question or says what was done.
- Report results with their numbers and how they were checked. Say plainly what failed, what was
  skipped and what was not verified.
- Keep a reply as long as its content. A reply under about fifteen lines needs no headings. Use a
  table only to compare things side by side.
- Give file references as `path:line`.
- Do not end with a summary of the reply. End on the last fact, or on the one decision left to
  the user.
