              Setting up the key, once
              
        On Windows, in PowerShell: ssh-keygen -t ed25519, then put the line in %USERPROFILE%\.ssh\id_ed25519.pub in the server's /root/.ssh/authorized_keys (your hosting provider's panel has a place for SSH keys). With a passphrase, Start-Service ssh-agent and ssh-add keep it unlocked while you are logged in.
