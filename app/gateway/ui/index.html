    Partners

    A partner is another gateway, named by callsign, that this one can reach by radio. When this gateway is cut off from the Internet, Forward now calls the partner and hands it the sessions waiting to be reported, with their mail; the partner reports them, or forwards them in turn. It also brings back the mail the partner holds for this gateway's users. Name each other on both gateways: a gateway answers forwarding calls from its partners only. Stop the gateway to add or remove one.

    Windows

    When partners may call this gateway: a channel, and a time that comes round every hour or every few hours. A cut-off partner calls only inside one, and this gateway calls its partners inside theirs. A window is published only inside a segment where your country's rules let an unattended station start a data transmission. Partners read your windows from your record at your post office, so register again in Setup after a change. Stop the gateway to add or remove one.

    Callers refused

    An account by its address, or a station by its callsign: a callsign without an SSID refuses every SSID of it. The caller is told this gateway's operator does not carry its sessions; your note stays here.
